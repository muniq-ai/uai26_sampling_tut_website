Scalable Sampling of Bayesian Neural Networks

Recent methodological, theoretical, and software advances that make sampling feasible at practically relevant scales

Date August 17, 2026
Duration 3 Hours
Location Amsterdam, Netherlands
View Schedule

About the Tutorial

🎯

Objective

This tutorial provides a comprehensive overview of modern MCMC sampling approaches and recent advances making sampling-based posterior approximation feasible for modern neural network architectures. It will equip participants with the necessary theoretical and practical tools to turn their own architectures into Bayesian neural networks and to run scalable sampling routines to quantify uncertainty or investigate theoretical properties of their network.

👥

Requirements

Researchers and practitioners with basic deep learning knowledge. No prior background in Bayesian inference or sampling is required.

📚

What You'll Learn

From posterior geometry and Langevin dynamics to modern HMC variants, convergence diagnostics, and hands-on implementation using state-of-the-art sampling libraries.
